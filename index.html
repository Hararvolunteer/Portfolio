Our Founder

Founder's Message

A vision rooted in service, leadership, and a deep love for Harar and its people.

Engineer Sami Abdusetar

Founder, Harar-Jugol Youth Voluntary Initiative

«Welcome to the Harar-Jugol Youth Voluntary Initiative.

Our mission is to empower young people to lead positive change through volunteerism, education, environmental stewardship, water and sanitation (WASH), climate action, culture, heritage, language, and sustainable community development.

Together, we are preserving our identity, protecting our environment, and building a more inclusive, resilient, and sustainable future for Harar and beyond.

Empowering Youth • Preserving Heritage • Advancing Sustainable Change

— Engineer Sami Abdusetar Founder, Harar-Jugol Youth Voluntary Initiative

Our Work

Active Programs

Tangible projects making a real difference in the community every single day.

🧹

Community Clean-Up Campaigns

Regular clean-up drives across Harar neighborhoods to promote hygiene and civic pride.

🌲

Tree Planting

Planting thousands of trees annually to combat deforestation and beautify public spaces.

🏺

Heritage Preservation

Restoring historic sites and documenting Harar's rich cultural traditions for posterity.

🎒

School Support Programs

Providing supplies, tutoring, and infrastructure support to local schools in need.

🏥

Health Awareness Campaigns

Educating communities on preventive health, hygiene, and access to medical resources.

🎓

Youth Leadership Academy

Intensive training program developing skills in public speaking, project management, and civic engagement.

💻

Innovation Workshops

Hands-on sessions in technology, entrepreneurship, and creative problem-solving for youth.
